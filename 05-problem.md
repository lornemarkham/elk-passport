# Problem

People have limited free time.

Whether it's a free Saturday, a long weekend, or a once-in-a-lifetime vacation, every day matters.

Yet discovering experiences that are actually worth their limited time is surprisingly difficult.

## Why

Today's information is scattered across tourism websites, blogs, Google Maps, Facebook events, Instagram, Reddit, local news, and word of mouth.

The information is often:

- outdated
- incomplete
- generic
- driven by marketing
- missing practical details that matter

## The result

People often:

- stay home
- repeat the same activities
- miss hidden gems
- miss time-sensitive events
- waste valuable time researching
- exceed their budgets
- discover amazing places years after living nearby
- finish vacations wondering what they missed

## The real cost

The real cost is not money.

The real cost is missed memories, missed opportunities, and not making the most of the limited free time people have.

This product exists to remove the friction between having free time and having an unforgettable day.

## What this product is not

This product is NOT trying to become another tourism directory.

Its purpose is to help people confidently make the most of their limited free time.

People are not looking for more information. They are looking for better decisions.
