# Heroes

## Hero #1 — The Local Explorer

A busy person with limited free time who wants to make the most of their weekends, evenings, holidays, and days off.

They know there are amazing experiences nearby but don't know how to discover them easily.

### Biggest challenges

- routine
- decision fatigue
- lack of motivation
- limited free time
- not knowing what they are missing

### What they are looking for

Confidence that their limited free time is being well spent.

### What they want

- make the most of their weekends
- discover places they never knew existed
- create memorable experiences
- stay within budget
- reduce planning friction
- reset before another busy week
- try something new

### Priority

This is the first and primary hero for Version 1.

**Reason:** We are building for ourselves first. This allows us to validate the product every weekend using real adventures instead of assumptions.
