# Curation Framework

ELK Passport is not a search engine.

It is a trusted curator.

Rather than asking users where they want to go, Passport seeks to understand the person before recommending experiences.

The goal is not to maximize options.

The goal is to maximize confidence.

Passport combines four kinds of understanding:

## 1. Adventure DNA

Long-term preferences that rarely change.

Examples include:

- loves water
- dislikes water
- fear of heights
- history enthusiast
- foodie
- dog owner
- vegetarian
- budget conscious
- luxury traveler
- loves hidden gems

These are examples only, not a finalized model.

## 2. Today's Intent

What kind of day the person wants today.

Examples include:

- Adventure
- Active
- Family
- Food
- Water
- Learning
- Date
- Road Trip
- Escape
- Relax

These are examples only and will evolve.

## 3. Hard Constraints

Real-world limitations that should eliminate unsuitable recommendations before curation begins.

Examples include:

- available time
- budget
- maximum driving distance
- accessibility
- weather
- mobility
- open today

## 4. Context

Passport should consider relevant situational context.

Examples include:

- weather
- season
- local events
- time of day
- sunset
- location

## The output

The output is not hundreds of search results.

Passport should confidently recommend a flexible day that naturally fits the person's interests, preferences, budget, and circumstances.

The recommendation should feel adaptable rather than rigid.

The user should feel they can modify the day without losing the overall experience.

The recommendation should earn trust by demonstrating genuine understanding.

A successful recommendation creates the reaction:

"Of course. That's exactly what I wanted."

Poor recommendations immediately damage trust.

The product should prioritize understanding the user over maximizing the number of recommendations.

## The goal

Reduce decision fatigue.

Increase anticipation.

Help people spend less time searching and more time creating memorable days.
