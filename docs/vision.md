# Vision

_Living document. Source of truth: the Product Brain (`/01-mission.md` through `/12-mvp-experience-visual-contract.md`) at the repository root. This document is a synthesis for engineering purposes — if it ever disagrees with the Product Brain, the Product Brain wins._

## What ELK Passport is

ELK Passport is **not** an itinerary planner. It is an experience platform that helps people make the most of their free time by curating a day made up of experiences they are genuinely delighted to see. See [`/01-mission.md`](../01-mission.md), [`/10-core-experience.md`](../10-core-experience.md).

## Mission

Help people create unforgettable memories through real adventures — for people exploring close to home and people travelling somewhere new. See [`/01-mission.md`](../01-mission.md).

## The problem

People have limited free time, and discovering experiences actually worth that time is surprisingly difficult. Today's information is scattered, outdated, generic, and marketing-driven. The result: people stay home, repeat the same activities, miss hidden gems and time-sensitive events, and waste time researching.

The real cost is not money — it is missed memories and missed opportunities. People aren't looking for more information; they're looking for better decisions. ELK Passport is explicitly **not** trying to become another tourism directory. See [`/05-problem.md`](../05-problem.md).

## The core experience

Everything in the product exists to support one capability:

> Passport curates a day made up of experiences the user is genuinely delighted to see.

It is not fundamentally an AI product, a maps product, a reviews product, a search product, a social product, or a gamification product — those may become valuable capabilities later, but they are not the core. Every future feature is evaluated against one question: **does this help Passport curate a more delightful day?** See [`/10-core-experience.md`](../10-core-experience.md).

## Success

Success is a life outcome, not a business metric — not downloads, revenue, or engagement. Success looks like people saying things like _"I actually look forward to weekends again"_ or _"My family still talks about that day."_ See [`/07-success.md`](../07-success.md).

## Principles

Two principles documents currently exist in the Product Brain and have not yet been reconciled: [`/02-principles.md`](../02-principles.md) (5 principles) and [`/08-principles.md`](../08-principles.md) (10 principles, more detailed). Engineers should treat `08-principles.md` as the more current and complete set until the founder reconciles the two.

Recurring themes across both, most relevant to engineering decisions:

- **People over technology** — technology exists to create better experiences, not to impress people.
- **Curated over comprehensive** — fifty unforgettable experiences beat five thousand average ones.
- **Less planning, more living** — reduce decision fatigue.
- **Trust is earned** — every recommendation should strengthen trust; poor recommendations damage it immediately.
- **Keep it fun** — never let the product become work, homework, or a lecture.
