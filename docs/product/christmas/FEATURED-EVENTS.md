# Featured events — Christmas in Vernon (M1152)

`src/domain/christmas/featured.ts` is a short, hand-checked list of events
that must be visible on the day, decided purely by the clock (Pacific).
Ended events leave Tonight on their own. Atlas remains the home of events:
the ordinary feed drops its own copy of a featured event (`matchesFeatured`)
so nobody sees a card twice. Delete an entry once Atlas carries it properly.
