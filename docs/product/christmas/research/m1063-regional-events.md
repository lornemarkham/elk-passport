# Regional Christmas / holiday-season events, 2026–27 (Nov 23 2026 – Jan 3 2027)

Research date: 2026-11-23. Read-only web research for M1063.

**Status rule:** I mark an event **VERIFIED** only when I read an official or organizer page myself (or the organizer's ticketing page) and it printed the 2026 date, or explicitly the 2026–27 season. Everything else is **UNVERIFIED**, and the note says why. Dates, times, addresses and prices are copied as printed. Where a page printed no street address I have written "(no address printed)" rather than fill one in.

**Sites I could not read directly:**

- vernon.ca, kelowna.ca, theatre.kelowna.ca (Kelowna Community Theatre), landmarkdistrict.ca (WebFetch only) and castanet.net all returned HTTP 403.
- tourismvernon.com listing pages render client-side, so they came back empty. Some of its event detail pages did load.
- skisilverstar.com event pages loaded only the title, date and venue. No times or costs came through.
- members.downtownvernon.com returned 403.
- I found no 2026 posting on coldstream.ca, the Salmon Arm city calendar (apart from two small events), the Lake Country site (its events URL returned 404) or the Lumby site.

Ticket Seller (ticketseller.ca) is the box office of the Vernon & District Performing Arts Centre Society. I treat it as the organizer's ticketing page.
