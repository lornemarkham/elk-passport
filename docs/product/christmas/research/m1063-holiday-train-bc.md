# CPKC Holiday Train 2026: British Columbia schedule (rebuilt without cpkcr.com)

Researched 2026-11-23, read-only. The official page https://www.cpkcr.com/en/community/HolidayTrain sits behind a bot challenge and was **not** accessed or bypassed.

## Primary source found (CPKC-official)

**CPKC's own published schedule PDF**, served from CPKC's GIS host (no bot challenge):
https://gis.cpkcr.com/arcgis/rest/directories/arcgisoutput/holidaytrain/canada_holiday_train_route.pdf

- Header: "Canada Holiday Train Schedule / Nov 21 - Dec 20, 2026". Columns: Day, Event Time, City/Town, Address, Artist.
- PDF metadata: created by Esri ArcGIS, CreationDate **Fri Nov 20 2026 06:09 MST**. So this is the current 2026 file. The URL has no year in it and may be overwritten each season, so re-check it before relying on it.
- The PDF does not give a time zone. Times appear to be local. Golden prints 14:15 and Revelstoke 18:30 on the same day, which fits Golden being on Mountain time and Revelstoke on Pacific. That is an inference, not printed.
- The PDF gives one "Event Time" window per stop. It does not separate train arrival from performance. Treat the window as the show window. The train usually arrives a few minutes before.

**CPKC 2026 news release** (PR Newswire, "SOURCE CPKC", dateline "CALGARY, AB, Oct. 13, 2026"): "CPKC Holiday Train begins 182-stop journey of music and giving Nov. 19". I read it through the syndicated copy at https://finviz.com/news/401975/cpkc-holiday-train-begins-182-stop-journey-of-music-and-giving-nov-19 (also on Webull).

- It names Sam Roberts Band and Owen Riegling among 12 artists. It does not say which artists play which region and does not list BC stops.
- Donation ask, printed: CPKC "encourages attendees to bring a cash donation or non-perishable food item", with "all contributions staying in the local community to help families in need."
- Safety, printed: stay 15 metres (50 feet) back from the tracks, and never stand on the track or ballast for photos.
- **It does not state that admission is free.**

### Per-stop detail

**1. Revelstoke, Dec 16**

- Location: "Victoria Road". This is a street name only. No street number or landmark is printed for 2026. Past coverage puts the stop near the downtown CPKC yard and Revelstoke Railway Museum on Victoria Road, but no 2026 source says so.
- Official event URL: the cpkcr.com page (not readable) and the CPKC PDF. I found no 2026 page on revelstoke.ca or from Revelstoke Community Connections.
- Free admission: not printed in any 2026 source I read for this stop.
- Food bank: UNVERIFIED for 2026. In 2024 the beneficiary was the Community Connections Revelstoke Society (CCRS) food bank, with fire-rescue hot-dog sales and donation boxes (Revelstoke Mountaineer, news). CCRS is the likely beneficiary again, but that is not confirmed.
- Confidence: schedule VERIFIED (CPKC). Beneficiary UNVERIFIED.
- Conflict, resolved: a Castanet article says "Wednesday, Dec. 17 ... Revelstoke at 2:45 p.m., Victoria Street". Dec 17 was a Wednesday in **2025**, not 2026, so this is last year's schedule. Use the 2026 CPKC time, 18:30.

**2. Sicamous, Dec 17**

- Location: "Highway 1 & Silver Sand Road". This is an intersection with no street address printed.
- Food bank: UNVERIFIED for 2026. Historically the Eagle Valley Community Support Society food bank (Eagle Valley News, news, earlier years). The 2026 sicamous.ca news post (https://sicamous.ca/live-here/news/post/cpkc-holiday-train2) returned HTTP 403, so it was not read.
- Free admission: not printed in a 2026 source I could read.
- Confidence: schedule VERIFIED (CPKC). Beneficiary UNVERIFIED.

**3. Canoe, Dec 17**

- Location: "50 Street & Canoe Beach Drive". This is an intersection near Canoe Beach (Salmon Arm). No street number.
- Food bank: UNVERIFIED for 2026. Historically the Salvation Army Care and Share Centre (Canoe and Salmon Arm), per Shuswap Scoop and Castanet (news, earlier years). I found no CSRD page. Canoe is within the City of Salmon Arm, not CSRD.
- Confidence: schedule VERIFIED. Beneficiary UNVERIFIED.

**4. Salmon Arm, Dec 17**

- Location: "51 Lake Shore Drive NE". This is a street address. Earlier coverage says "Lakeshore Drive NE" (Castanet, year unclear).
- The salmonarm.ca calendar for Nov and Dec 2026 was checked and has **no Holiday Train entry**.
- Food bank: UNVERIFIED for 2026. Historically the Salvation Army. In 2025 CPKC presented a $3,000 cheque to the Salmon Arm Salvation Army at the Canoe stop (news).
- Confidence: schedule VERIFIED. Beneficiary UNVERIFIED.

**5. Notch Hill, Dec 17**

- Location: "1639 Notch Hill Road". This is a street address. Notch Hill is in CSRD Electoral Area C, near Sorrento.
- No CSRD or local 2026 page found. Food bank: UNVERIFIED, and I found no historical beneficiary either.
- Confidence: schedule VERIFIED. Beneficiary UNVERIFIED.

**6. Chase, Dec 18**

- Location: "Shuswap Avenue & Ash Drive". This is an intersection with no street number. 2024 and 2025 schedules used the same location and the same 14:45-15:15 window.
- No 2026 Village of Chase page found. Food bank: UNVERIFIED for 2026. Historically the Chase Christmas Hamper Society (Shuswap news, earlier years).
- Confidence: schedule VERIFIED. Beneficiary UNVERIFIED.
- Note: one search summary claimed Chase was "not on the 2026 BC list". That is wrong. The CPKC PDF lists it.

**7. Kamloops, Dec 18**

- Location, CPKC: "Sandman Center - Lorne Street". Tourism Kamloops 2026 (https://tourismkamloops.com/events/cpkc-holiday-train) prints "Sandman Centre Parking Lot", "300 Lorne Street", so a street address exists.
- Tourism Kamloops, 2026, printed: "December 18, 2026", "4:30pm", "Train arrival & concert times may vary slightly", "Sam Roberts Band and Owen Riegling", "FREE to watch, food bank donations encouraged.", "CPKC donates to the local food bank at each stop", and "encourages all attendees to make a monetary or heart-healthy food donation". Tourism Kamloops is the city's destination-marketing organisation, not the City itself. I label it **tourism-official**.
- City of Kamloops page https://www.kamloops.ca/node/10068 is the **2023** listing (Sat Dec 16 2023, Tyler Shaw and Kiesza, "Sandman Center, rear parking lot", "Bring a non-perishable food bank donation for the Kamloops Food Bank"). It is stale and not 2026.
- Food bank: the Kamloops Food Bank is the historical beneficiary (City, 2023). For 2026 the beneficiary is not named; Tourism Kamloops says only "local food bank".
- **Conflict:** CPKC gives 17:00-17:30, Tourism Kamloops gives 4:30pm. The likely reason is that 4:30 is arrival or gathering time and 17:00 is the show (2023 City: "4:45 - 5:30pm, performance starting at 5:00 pm"). Use CPKC's 17:00-17:30 as the show window.
- Confidence: schedule VERIFIED (CPKC, plus tourism-official). Free admission VERIFIED (tourism-official). Beneficiary named only generically.

## Free admission summary

Free admission is printed for Kamloops (Tourism Kamloops 2026) and Metro Vancouver (Destination Vancouver). The CPKC release and PDF do **not** print the word "free". For the other stops, free admission is customary but UNVERIFIED in 2026 text.

## Food-bank summary

No 2026 official source names a beneficiary for any priority stop. The CPKC-wide ask is "a cash donation or non-perishable food item", which stays local. The historical beneficiaries above come from news or older official pages. Treat them as UNVERIFIED for 2026.
