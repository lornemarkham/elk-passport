**The gap between 82 and 1.5 is the main finding about the critic itself.**
The rules catch _defects_: closed, unheard, misprinted. They don't catch a day
that is defect-free and still pointless, such as two playgrounds and a beach
park on a rainy afternoon. That is why judgements are kept, signed and
current in this harness, and why the scores must not be read as quality. Do
not tune the rules until one person's judgements, as well as this agent's,
exist to tune them against.

**What `/go` asks Atlas for:** nothing about the day and nothing about age.
`src/app/go/page.tsx` calls `listDiscoveryCandidates(childAge)` with no `on`,
and `childAge` only comes from a `?age=` URL parameter, never from the
sentence. So the builder's own rule, _"a stated schedule that excludes the
day"_ (`build.ts`), never fires in production. The rule is there and the
input it needs never arrives.

## The five most valuable improvements

Ranked by how much of the gap between 82 and 1.5 each one closes, on this
evidence. Each one names how the critic will show that it worked. None is
approved by being listed here. Items 1 to 4 edit `/go` files, which the
roadmap says must wait for INT-5.

### 1. Ask Atlas about the day, and prefer what is on

- **Evidence:**
  - `/go` never sends `on=`, so the rink Atlas already knows is out of season
    is recommended twice.
  - Atlas says 15–19 things in `/go`'s own pool are on today inside each day's
    circle. Of the 28 stops the ten days recommend, 2 are on that list. It includes:
    - near Vernon: U-Pick Pumpkins, Thanksgiving weekend at Davison Orchards
      (2 km), O'Keefe Ranch, Lincoln Lanes bowling;
    - near Vancouver: _Every River Has a Mouth_ (200 m from downtown), the
      Maritime Museum, _The Sunday Service_ (comedy, 7 p.m. tonight).
- **Change:**
  - Pass the day to `listDiscoveryCandidates` in `src/app/go/page.tsx` and
    `src/app/go/print/page.tsx`. That is two lines, and the builder's
    existing exclusion starts working.
  - Then give "on that day" weight in `fitness`, since Atlas states it. That
    is evidence, not invention.
- **The critic will show:**
  - `pageAsked.on` becomes true;
  - `stop.not-on-date` goes to 0;
  - `onDayUsed` rises.
- **It will not fix** the Allan Brooks Nature Centre, because Atlas holds no
  hours for it. That is item 5.

### 2. Hear the whole sentence

- **Evidence:** 10 of the 21 findings are about reading the sentence.
  - _"three **free** hours"_: a word between the number and the unit.
  - _"an hour or two"_ is read as a one-hour day.
  - _"my kids, they're 5 and 8"_ and _"3 and 6"_: ages after the noun.
  - _"downtown"_, _"evening"_ and _"colleagues"_ have no field in `DayBrief`.
- **Change:**
  - Make `readBrief` tolerate these forms. When several ages are given, take
    the youngest: it is the binding constraint, and it was said.
  - Add a time of day and a "keep it central" reach to `DayBrief`.
  - Carry a sentence's age to Atlas (`childAge`), as `?age=` already does.
- **This touches `DayBrief`,** which every `/go` branch shares. Who's coming
  ("colleagues") overlaps doctrine §2.1 and §15 Q12. Read it as a stated
  fact; don't infer a group profile from it.
- **The critic will show:** `reading.*` findings fall from 10, and relevance
  rises.

### 3. A venue isn't a destination without something on

- **Evidence:**
  - The Queen Elizabeth Theatre leads three Vancouver days, including the
    young family's and the meeting day. Its own operator says there is no
    access between performances, and no show was found that day.
  - Priest Valley Arena is a club venue with scheduled public skates only.
- **Change:** theatres, arenas, stadiums and similar venue subtypes become
  stops only through an occurrence there on the day (Atlas holds `happens_at`
  venues for events), never on their own.
- **Depends on item 1** for the day.
- **The critic will show:** the ledger flags fewer stops "with caveat". Add a
  scenario for "what's on tonight".

### 4. Rain means indoors, and "far" means far

- **Evidence:**
  - Rain: the builder reads only Atlas's rain reading, which covers 154
    subjects. It ignores `exposure`, which covers 2,088 and reads 162 placed
    things near Vernon as indoor. The rainy-day answers were two parks and a
    beach park, and three parks across three municipalities.
  - Distance: fitness always rewards closeness (`6 × (1 − d/r)`), so "worth a
    longer drive" can't produce a long drive even when it is read.
  - "Unusual" means few stated verbs, so it rewards thin records.
- **Change:**
  - Use `exposure` after the rain reading for an indoor brief, as
    `lane/discovery` `9582d62` already does for Discovery.
  - For "far" and "unusual", reuse that branch's `drive` and rarity lenses
    rather than building a second ranking: the roadmap and Gold Bank GB-025
    say _"do not build a second discovery engine"_.
- **The critic will show:**
  - `need.sheltered` holds with fewer _derived_ caveats;
  - `need.worth-the-drive` passes;
  - the judgement of the unusual day moves.

### 5. Feed Atlas what the paper got wrong

- **Evidence:** four defects that only Atlas can fix:
  - Allan Brooks Nature Centre's Tue–Sat hours aren't in Atlas;
  - the Sun Yat-Sen garden's coordinates are about 400 m off;
  - the Okanagan Science Centre is held twice;
  - parks carry no locality, so the paper prints no town.

  The ledger found them in one pass of 17 places.

- **Change:** hand the ledger to M172's entity-quality system as automated
  findings. Its shape (entity, reason, sources) is already the one doctrine
  §15 Q15 describes.

  Then make the ledger a standing step: each critic run lists the
  `unverified` stops it met, and a bounded research job checks only those.
  That stays cheap: today the ledger is 17 entries.

- **This doesn't answer §15 Q15** (who may flag, what it is called). It uses
  the automated-findings half M172 was asked to build.
- **The critic will show:** `stop.real-world.*` findings fall, `dateKnown`
  rises, and the share of verified stops stays high as days change.
