## 4. Vancouver data coverage (production pool, 2026-10-24)

From `verification/evidence/atlas-audit-2026-10-24.md`:

- 1,966 Metro Vancouver subjects (729 Place, 1,184 Organization, 53 Event);
  1,541 within 2 km of the Parq. ~700 restaurants, ~320 cafés, ~120 bars/pubs.
- Opening hours: good for food and drink near the Parq (151/188
  restaurants, 72/87 cafés, 26/39 bars within 1 km); poor for venues (2/41
  theatres/cinemas, 7/27 museums).
- **Images:** within 2 km, 76% show an Atlas _category_ stock photo (44
  URLs across 1,421 subjects — every park, including Stanley Park, shows a
  New York pier). Cards label these "Illustrative photo · author, licence",
  so nothing is passed off as the venue; it still looks generic.
- **Descriptions:** 72% empty (89% for food, drink and venues).
- **Events:** almost nothing dated Oct 26–29 near the Parq; no Canucks
  games; Rogers Arena/BC Place "events" are historical (2010 Olympics…).
- 39% of the whole pool has no location; 12 exact Place/Organization twins
  in Vancouver; inconsistent subtypes (Bar/bar, Museum/Gallery).

These are Atlas fixes (the audit lists the ten highest-impact ones). Passport
already degrades honestly: illustrative photos are labelled, "No hours
published — check before you go" is said, unplaced subjects are not offered
downtown.

## 5. Remaining defects and open owner questions

1. **gohaveaday.com with no location mixes Okanagan and Vancouver, and
   shows Vernon's weather.** The corpus is 1,870 Metro Vancouver to 987
   Okanagan, short of the 2× majority `dominantArea` requires, and ADR 002
   defines the default as the corpus's area. Options for the owner: (a)
   derive the default _area_ (never a coordinate) from the request's
   country/city headers — a new kind of location under ADR 002; (b) ask for
   a town when nothing is known (ADR 002's own open question); (c) leave it.
   **Until decided, share links with `?at=jw-marriott-parq`** —
   `https://gohaveaday.com/?at=jw-marriott-parq` opens Discovery measured
   from the hotel (once this candidate is deployed — the id is new).
2. **"Start over" keeps the rows**, including an activity a sentence set:
   after "a restaurant for five", Start over then "something fun within a
   walk" still answers with restaurants. Deliberate and tested today (the
   open question recorded with ADR 006); worth deciding before people use it.
3. **Homepage weight:** ~830 KB compressed (7.4 MB HTML) and a 7.5 s cold
   load measured on production — the whole pool goes to the browser.
4. Bar width at 320 px: even folded, the bar scrolls sideways to reach
   Experiences (it scrolls; nothing is lost).
5. A 45-minute window can still offer an 80-minute show: Atlas states that
   duration only in prose, not as `visitDuration`.
6. Conference's new "Ask Go Have A Day" section is unit-tested, not
   browser-tested here (Conference's own specs need its `next dev` setup).
