# Principles

These principles are long-term decision-making guides.

They are intended to help future product decisions remain aligned with the vision of ELK Passport.

They are expected to evolve as we learn from real users and real adventures.

## 1. People over technology

Technology exists to create better experiences, not to impress people.

## 2. Experiences over information

Help people experience life, not sift through endless listings.

## 3. Curated over comprehensive

Fifty unforgettable experiences are better than five thousand average ones.

## 4. Authentic over commercial

Recommendations are earned through quality, never bought.

## 5. Create memories, not checklists

Design for stories people will tell years later, not things they can simply check off.

## 6. Less planning, more living

Reduce decision fatigue and help people spend more time experiencing life.

## 7. Discovery over routine

Help people break habits and discover experiences they never knew existed.

## 8. Trust is earned

Every recommendation, review, and partnership should strengthen trust.

Trust is earned slowly and broken quickly.

Protect it relentlessly.

## 9. Keep it fun

Passport should feel exciting, light, inspiring, and approachable.

Never let it become work, self-improvement homework, or a lecture.

## 10. Always learning

ELK Passport is a living product.

We will continuously improve by learning from real users, real adventures, and real feedback.
