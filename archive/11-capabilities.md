# Capabilities

The [[10-core-experience|Core Experience]] established that Passport exists to curate a day made up of experiences the user is genuinely delighted to see.

To accomplish that, Passport requires a small number of fundamental capabilities.

A capability is something the product must be able to do. It is not a feature, a screen, a technical implementation, or a roadmap item.

## Capability 1 — Understand Me

Passport must understand the person's long-term preferences, interests, dislikes, values, and Adventure DNA.

Examples include:

- interests
- fears
- dietary preferences
- activity preferences
- travel style
- budget tendencies

The capability is understanding the person, not collecting data.

## Capability 2 — Understand Today

Passport must understand today's intent and circumstances.

This includes things like:

- desired type of day
- available time
- budget
- weather
- location
- season
- other relevant context

The capability is understanding today's situation.

## Capability 3 — Curate My Day

Passport combines its understanding of the person and today's circumstances to confidently recommend a delightful day.

The recommendation should feel personal rather than generic.

The recommendation should prioritize confidence over quantity.

## Capability 4 — Adapt My Day

A great day should remain flexible.

If plans change, weather changes, someone becomes tired, or circumstances shift, Passport should adapt the recommendation while preserving the spirit of the day.

The recommendation should bend instead of breaking.

## Together

These four capabilities together deliver the Core Experience.

Future capabilities may emerge over time, but every future capability should strengthen one or more of these core capabilities.
