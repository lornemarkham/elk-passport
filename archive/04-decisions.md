# Decisions

- Badges, achievements, gamification, passport stamps, and similar mechanics are valuable future capabilities, but they are not required for the product to fulfill its core promise. The irreducible core experience of Passport is delightful curation (see [[10-core-experience]]) — a user should still be able to finish the day saying "That was a damn good day" even if none of those systems exist.
  - _Original decision (superseded):_ "Badges are a core part of the product." This is kept here for historical context. As the Product Brain matured through the Core Experience phase, we clarified that badges are a valuable layer on top of the product, not part of its irreducible core.
- Treasure Chest is the permanent home for future ideas.
- The product is designed for both locals and travellers.
