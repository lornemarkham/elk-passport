# Success

Success is not measured by downloads, revenue, or engagement.

Those are business metrics.

This document defines the life outcomes we hope to create for people.

We will know ELK Passport is succeeding when people naturally say things like:

- "ELK Passport changed the way I spend my free time."
- "I actually look forward to weekends again."
- "We discovered places we never knew existed."
- "My family still talks about that day."
- "I never thought I could have this much fun so close to home."
- "That became one of my favorite memories."
- "I recommended this adventure to all my friends."
- "That hidden restaurant served one of the best meals I've ever had."
- "This completely changed the way we travel."

These are examples of the kinds of outcomes we hope to create.

They are not marketing copy or guarantees.

Their purpose is to guide product decisions by reminding us that we are trying to improve people's lives, not simply build an application.
